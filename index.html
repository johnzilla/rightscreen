About

The 4th Amendment protects against unreasonable searches and seizures. This tool generates lockscreen images that clearly communicate your awareness of and commitment to these constitutional rights.

How to set your lockscreen:

  • iPhone: Settings > Wallpaper > Add New Wallpaper > Photos
  • Android: Settings > Wallpaper > Lock screen > My photos

RightScreen is provided for informational purposes. This is not legal advice.
